Causal Toolkit
Synthetic control
Did an event actually cause a stock to break from its peers, or is the "effect" just a pre-existing trend? Abadie-Diamond-Hainmueller synthetic control with a fit-quality gate that refuses to hand you a number it doesn't trust.
Notable result: BE's post-short-seller-report decline ranks last of its peer group in the placebo test — the honest read is "not a clear break," not the dramatic story a reader might expect.

Factor Decomposition
3-factor OLS
For each holding: how much of its return rode Market, Sector, or Rates — versus being genuinely stock-specific? Factors orthogonalized in sequence so betas are clean, not confounded. R² gated up front, not glossed over on low-confidence names.
Method note: log returns throughout make the split exactly additive — no compounding cross-terms to hand-wave away.
